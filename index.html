Crafted With Colombian Heart.
Empowered by Women Leadership.

Founded by master barber and cosmetologist Liliana, Colombian Barbershop & Beauty Salon was created to bring the warmth, precision, and world-renowned artistic grooming of Colombia into an elevated, modern sanctuary.

We proudly identify as a women-owned business, breaking conventions in the traditional grooming space. Our studio bridges the gap between a high-caliber gentleman's barbershop and a lavish beauty salon—offering an inclusive space where men, women, and families experience genuine craftsmanship, immaculate attention to detail, and authentic Latin warmth.

Uncompromising Precision

Every fade, scissor contour, and razor stroke is executed to perfection.

Warm Colombian Hospitality

Fresh tinto coffee, genuine camaraderie, and relaxation from the moment you enter.

Dual Sanctuary for Men & Women

From sharp fades and beard trims to silk blowouts and balayage under one roof.
